Complete Workflow for Manage Top Customers Invoice & Vendor Bill Analytics

Install and activate the Top Customers Invoice & Vendor Bill Analytics module from Odoo Apps. Go to Invoicing → Reporting → Top Customers/Vendors and select the required date range for invoice reporting. Choose the report type as Top Customers or Top Vendors, then generate reports based on highest invoice amounts, total orders, or quantities. Users can also apply invoice status filters and compare invoice analytics with previous periods for better business invoice analysis. The generated customer and vendor reports can be exported in PDF and Excel formats for accounting reporting and performance tracking.
